TWO INSTRUMENT CORRECTIONS, both recorded rather than quietly fixed:
 1. The fold's 0.873 overlap factor does NOT generalise here.  Borrowing it
    over-priced activate by 1.3x and put the first draft of the study's
    bracket at 23.9-25.4%.  Measured, the bracket is 20.8-24.4%.
 2. The first harness gave all three loads in a merge the SAME buffer
    offset, so they hit one cache line; the real kernel reads three separate
    arrays.  Distinct addresses: 0.532 -> 0.586 c/op.

The FOLD measurement (mix-fold.txt) does NOT share defect 2: its sequence has
exactly one memory operand per chunk-update and the offset advances per slot.

activate's 40 lane-iterations are mutually independent and LLVM unrolls by 12
(census: vpmullw appears 24x = 2/iteration x 12), so >=4 groups is the
engine's case.  The curve has not flattened at 4, so the study brackets
activate between the groups-4 and groups-3 readings (0.586-0.735) rather than
quoting the best number.
