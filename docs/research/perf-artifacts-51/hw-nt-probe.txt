tools/perf51/ntprobe51.c, gcc -O2 -march=native, taskset -c 3, 20000 iters
COLD chunk 24 KB (384 lines) from a 64 MB array; HOT array 24 KB (384 lines).

VERDICT
 A: VMOVNTDQA generates 7.81 M DEMAND L1 fills for 7.68 M lines.  It allocates
    in L1 exactly like a normal load.  The hw prefetcher contributed only 343 k,
    so the fills are the NT loads themselves -- not a prefetcher masking effect.
 B: hot-only costs 23,954 fills (0.003 per line: fully resident).  Under EVERY
    cold loader the total is 15.74-15.80 M, i.e. hot + cold with the hot array
    missing on every single access.  Excess over (cold-only + hot-only) is
    ~7.7 M = exactly the hot array's 7.68 M lines.
 => Neither VMOVNTDQA nor PREFETCHNTA provides ANY cache-pollution control on
    WB memory on Zen 4.  PREFETCHNTA additionally costs cycles (166.8 M, the
    worst of the four).  Commission mechanisms 1 and 2 are both closed.
